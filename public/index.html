Market Pulse

Pulls stock and crypto coverage from major outlets via direct RSS + Google News, spotlights anything from tracked figures, and shows standard technical indicators on delayed public price data.

Read this first. Not financial advice. Nothing here is a buy/sell recommendation. The confluence tally on each price card counts how many indicators lean the same direction — it is a count of lagging descriptive statistics, not a prediction or a signal. RSI/Stochastic/CCI/Williams %R in overbought territory lean down (price stretched high, less room above) — not up. Bollinger band touches are ambiguous and are excluded from directional counts. The "⚡ mover" flag and tone badges are keyword heuristics. The risk calculator does sizing math on prices you supply; it never picks an entry or exit. Prices are delayed — not real-time, not licensed market data. YouTube "Find video" results are unverified candidates. Nothing here places trades or connects to any broker.
