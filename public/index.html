Market Pulse

Pulls stock and crypto coverage from major outlets (via direct RSS + Google News aggregation) and, when configured, live X posts — then spotlights anything from your tracked figures, with video where it genuinely exists.

Read this first. Not financial advice, not a guarantee of completeness, and nothing here is a buy/sell recommendation. It can't tap into live TV broadcast audio — no consumer-accessible API exists for that. X posts show their real embedded video straight from X's own widget when a tracked account's post has one. The "Find video" button on any card searches YouTube for possible related clips — those are unverified candidates for you to check, never a confirmed match. The ⚡ "mover" flag is keyword-based, not sentiment analysis. The price panel shows standard technical indicators computed from delayed public data. The "Any-symbol tracker" below embeds TradingView's own live charts and their own Technical Analysis gauge (a mechanical average of standard indicators) for whatever symbol you add — that gauge is TradingView's, not this app's, and like every indicator it describes what already happened, not what happens next. Nothing in this app places trades or connects to MetaTrader or any broker.
